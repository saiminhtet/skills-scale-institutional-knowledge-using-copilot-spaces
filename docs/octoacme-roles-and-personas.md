# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works closely with QA/Testing Specialists** to ensure tests align with acceptance criteria
- **Collaborates with Technical Leads** on design reviews and architectural decisions
- **Receives project oversight from Project Managers** and priority guidance from Product Managers
- **Engages with Security Officers** on security implementation and scanning results

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Partners with Project Managers** on timelines and scope management
- **Guides Developers and Technical Leads** on acceptance criteria and product vision
- **Coordinates with Stakeholder/Sponsors** on business priorities and constraints
- **Works with QA/Testing Specialists** to define testable success criteria

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Manages team capacity** in coordination with Engineering Managers
- **Escalates risks and blockers** identified by Developers, QA, and Technical Leads
- **Reports to Stakeholder/Sponsors** and coordinates with Product Managers on priorities
- **Integrates Security Officer** concerns into risk registers and mitigation plans

---

## QA/Testing Specialist

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Specialists own quality assurance and acceptance validation throughout the project lifecycle. They collaborate with product and development teams to define testable criteria and ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Validate features against Definition of Done
- Identify and report defects with clear reproduction steps
- Participate in test strategy planning during project planning phase
- Support smoke testing before releases

### Goals
- Ensure consistent quality and user experience
- Catch issues early to reduce production incidents
- Build confidence in features before release

### Typical Communication
- Participation in sprint planning and kick-off meetings
- Test plan reviews with product and development leads
- Defect reports in project tracking system
- QA sign-off before release

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Collaborates with Developers** to understand technical implementation and define testable criteria
- **Works with Product Managers** to clarify acceptance criteria and success metrics
- **Coordinates with Security Officers** on security testing requirements
- **Reports blockers to Project Managers** that impact release readiness
- **Partners with Technical Leads** on test strategy for complex features

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural direction and technical risk assessment. They guide design decisions, conduct technical reviews, and ensure solutions align with system-wide goals.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical design for complex features
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Guide team on technology choices and best practices
- Assist in capacity planning for technically complex work
- Mentor developers on architectural patterns

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce rework and technical debt
- Support team capability growth

### Typical Communication
- Design review discussions
- Technical spike documentation
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Risk register input for technical concerns

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Mentors Developers** on design patterns and best practices
- **Collaborates with Security Officers** on architectural security considerations
- **Works with QA/Testing Specialists** to ensure testability of complex designs
- **Provides risk input to Project Managers** for planning and mitigation
- **Reports to Engineering Managers** on technical team capacity and growth needs

---

## Security Officer

### Role Summary
Security Officers manage security reviews, threat assessment, and incident response protocols. They ensure projects meet compliance requirements and security standards.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security reviews of design and implementation
- Assess security risks in project planning
- Define security testing requirements
- Lead security incident response when needed
- Maintain security documentation and playbooks

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and breaches
- Ensure compliance with organizational standards
- Build secure systems by default

### Typical Communication
- Security review participation in planning
- Security scanning results and remediation tracking
- Incident response coordination
- Security training and best practices documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Reviews designs with Technical Leads** early in planning phase
- **Defines security acceptance criteria with Product Managers** and QA/Testing Specialists
- **Guides Developers** on secure implementation practices
- **Escalates security risks to Project Managers** and Stakeholder/Sponsors
- **Coordinates with Engineering Managers** on security training and awareness

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic alignment, and approvals for projects. They ensure initiatives align with organizational goals and priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals and success metrics for initiatives
- Provide strategic context and business constraints
- Review and approve project scope and timelines
- Make trade-off decisions between competing priorities
- Communicate project value across the organization

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between projects and organizational strategy
- Minimize risks to business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Project approval and prioritization decisions
- Escalation discussions for business-impacting issues
- Release announcements and business impact reporting

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Partners with Product Managers** on vision alignment and strategic priorities
- **Reviews project plans with Project Managers** and approves scope and timelines
- **Receives risk escalations from Project Managers** for business-impacting decisions
- **Works with Technical Leads** to understand feasibility and technical constraints
- **Engages with Security Officers** on compliance and regulatory requirements

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers supervise engineers, manage team capacity, and support career development. They ensure team health, capability, and effective escalation.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity and resource allocation
- Support developer growth and skill development
- Escalate team-level blockers and issues to Project Managers
- Conduct performance reviews and career planning
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration

### Goals
- Build a high-performing, motivated engineering team
- Reduce bottlenecks and technical debt
- Enable team members to deliver quality work efficiently

### Typical Communication
- One-on-ones with team members
- Team retrospectives and skill development discussions
- Escalation coordination with Project Managers
- Capacity planning and resource allocation meetings

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Supervises Developers** and supports their growth and performance
- **Coordinates capacity with Project Managers** during planning
- **Works with Technical Leads** on mentoring and skill development
- **Supports Security Officers** in ensuring team compliance training
- **Engages with QA/Testing Specialists** on testing resource allocation
- **Escalates technical concerns and team risks** to Project Managers and Product Managers
